Privacy Policy

AskNiyati (com.antara.astrologer) · Last updated: 14 October 2026

AskNiyati ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI astrology companion operated by Reliable MediaX. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account details – when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a profile identifier.
  • Birth details – date, time and place of birth that you enter to generate your chart.
  • Your messages – the questions you type to AskNiyati and the replies generated.
  • Technical data – crash reports and basic device information (device model, OS version, app version) used to find and fix bugs.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or payment card details.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your Kundali (birth chart) and generate AI replies, horoscopes, predictions, remedies and match reports.
  • To create and manage your account and keep it linked to your Google sign-in.
  • To send an optional daily horoscope reminder notification, if you turn it on. This reminder is scheduled on your device.
  • To detect crashes and improve app stability.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Where your information is stored

Your birth details and chat history are stored on your device. Your account record (account identifier, email and usage status) is stored on our secure backend so that it stays linked to your Google account.

4. Third-party services that process data

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication – to sign you in securely.
  • AI provider (DeepSeek) – to generate replies, the text of your question and the relevant birth details are sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No human astrologer reads your messages.
  • Backend hosting and database (Supabase and MongoDB Atlas) – to store your account record.
  • Sentry – crash and error reporting.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us and under their own privacy policies.

5. Data security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to our backend is restricted and requires a verified sign-in. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Your choices and data deletion

From Settings → Privacy & Your Data in the app you can export your data, clear chat history, clear AI memory, or permanently delete your account and data. You can also request deletion by email; see how to delete your account. Deleted account data is removed from our backend; backups, if any, are purged within 30 days.

7. Data retention

We keep your account record for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, the associated data is deleted as described above.

8. Children

AskNiyati is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Entertainment and guidance only

Readings are AI-generated for reflection and entertainment. They are not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
