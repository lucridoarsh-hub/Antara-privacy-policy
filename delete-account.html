Delete your AskNiyati account

AskNiyati (com.antara.astrologer) · Last updated: 14 October 2026

You can delete your AskNiyati account and the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1 – In the app (instant)

  1. Open AskNiyati and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2 – By email

Email YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL from the Google address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my AskNiyati account". We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record on our backend (account identifier, email, usage status).
  • Your birth details, chat history and AI memory stored in the app.

What may be kept

Anonymous crash logs that contain no personal identifiers may be kept for a short period. Backups, if any, are purged within 30 days.
